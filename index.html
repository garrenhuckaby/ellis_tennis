Why Choose Ellis Tennis?

About Our Program

Hello! I'm Ellis, a certified tennis instructor with over 10 years of experience working with youth athletes. My passion is making tennis fun and accessible for kids of all skill levels, ages 6-16.

I believe that small group lessons create the perfect balance between individual attention and peer learning. With just 10 kids per group, every student gets quality instruction while building friendships with other young athletes.

My teaching philosophy focuses on fundamentals, confidence, and having fun on the court. Whether your child is picking up a racquet for the first time or working to improve their game, we create a supportive environment where every player can grow.
