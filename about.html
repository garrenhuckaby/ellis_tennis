About Us

Thatcher

"For years, most of the summer sunrises saw my uncle and I out hitting before work began for the day. He drilled my serve, critiqued my backhand and offered footwork advice. These are some of my favorite memories."

Tennis wasn't just a sport for Thatcher—it was a foundation for family bonding, work ethic, and community service. He believes in the power of the game to build character, not just technical skill.

"Tennis was how I spent time with family, learned to work, and served in my community."

As a coach, Thatcher focuses on sharing the joy and intrinsic rewards that tennis brings. He creates a supportive environment where young players discover the game's true value—the connections it builds, the discipline it teaches, and the confidence it instills. Skill development is important, but it's secondary to the life lessons tennis offers.

Eric

"When I was 14, I smashed my tennis racket after losing a key point in an important match."

That moment became a turning point. His father stepped in with a powerful lesson: emotional control is everything. If Eric couldn't manage his emotions on the court, he wouldn't be playing at all.

18 years of playing experience

Now, with nearly two decades of competitive experience, Eric is passionate about passing this wisdom to the next generation. He believes tennis is far more than a sport—it's one of life's best classrooms.

"It's one of the best ways to build discipline, resilience, and confidence."

"Learning to compete, handle pressure, and stay composed are skills that carry far beyond the court." Eric works with each student to develop not just their forehand or serve, but their ability to face challenges with poise and determination—qualities that define success in tennis and in life.
